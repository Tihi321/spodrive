Spodrive Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11

Spodrive is a music player for audio files in your own Google Drive. It runs entirely in your browser as a static web app, at spodrive.tihomir-selak.from.hr. There is no Spodrive server and no Spodrive account.

What data the app accesses

You sign in with Google and grant the drive.file scope. With it, Spodrive can access only:

  • the audio files and folders you choose in the Google Picker, and
  • the Spodrive folder the app creates in your Drive, which holds the library index and playlists (library.json, playlists.json).

It cannot see the rest of your Drive. Your original files are never modified or moved. Spodrive reads tags and cover art from your files in the browser and streams the audio for playback.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Spodrive does not collect, store, sell or share your data on any server of ours. It has no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps data in your browser (IndexedDB and Cache Storage): the library index, playlists, settings, cached covers and lyrics, and any tracks you download for offline use. Offline downloads are stored on your device unencrypted. Signing out in the app clears this data.

Third parties

  • Google (Sign-In, Drive API, Picker API): your audio and the library files live in your own Drive and are handled under Google's privacy policy.
  • lrclib.net: to show lyrics, the app sends a track's title, artist, album and duration to lrclib.net. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Netlify: hosts the static files of the app. If you use “Sign in with a code” on a TV or smart display, the sign-in passes through a Spodrive function on Netlify. It only forwards the OAuth exchange with Google (including the tokens Google returns), does not store or log it, and is used for nothing else.

Revoking access and deleting data

Use Sign out in the app to clear local data. To revoke Spodrive's access to your Google account, go to Google Account → Security → Third-party access (“Your connections to third-party apps & services”) and remove Spodrive. You can delete the Spodrive folder from your Drive at any time.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Spodrive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the music player features you see in the app, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.
